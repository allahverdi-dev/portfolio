Frequently asked questions

How much does a website cost?

Landing pages start at $99, business websites at $249, premium websites at $399, and redesigns at $100. The final quote depends on the agreed scope.

How do payments work?

Most projects use 50% upfront and 50% before final handover. Payment methods are agreed before work begins.

How many revisions are included?

Two revision rounds are included. Major scope changes are quoted separately.

Do you provide hosting?

I can deploy the website and help you choose a suitable host. Paid hosting is separate unless otherwise agreed.

Do you provide domains?

I can help with setup, but domain registration and renewal remain separate and should be owned by you.

Who provides website content?

You provide the final business information, text, brand assets and images. Content upload or basic structuring can be added to the scope.

Do you use AI to build websites?

Yes. AI supports research, implementation and iteration, while the finished work still goes through review, testing, debugging and refinement.

Can you redesign an existing website?

Yes. I can improve an outdated or difficult-to-use website while keeping the focus on your business goals and existing content.

I'll review your project details and get back to you as soon as possible.
